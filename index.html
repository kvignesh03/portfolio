Full-Stack Architecture

Hospital Appointment Booking System

Role-Based Access Separation (RBAC)

The system guarantees strict boundary protection between 3 user tiers:

  • Patient Role: Browse available doctor specialties, view open time slots, book appointments, and view personal consultation histories.
  • Doctor Role: Manage daily consultation availability slots, review patient booking requests, and record diagnosis notes.
  • Administrator Role: Master oversight of doctor onboarding, patient registrations, schedule auditing, and departmental analytics.

Database Normalization & Efficiency

Designed the relational schema in Third Normal Form (3NF), separating entity schemas for Users, Doctor_Schedules, Appointments, and Departments. This eliminated data redundancy, avoided booking collisions, and reduced query latency during peak scheduling times.
